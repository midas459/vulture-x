# VULTURE-X — Hybrid VTOL Research

VULTURE-X is an educational aerospace engineering concept exploring a hybrid vertical-takeoff-and-landing (VTOL) airframe with a lifting-body design language.

## Research scope
- Concept CAD and visual packaging
- High-level aerodynamic principles and stability concepts
- Benign simulation and documentation
- Civil aviation safety, operating constraints, and test planning
- Non-flight mockups and simulation-first validation

## Safety boundaries
This repository is limited to educational, civil, non-harmful aerospace study. It does not provide weaponization, payload delivery for harm, attack capability, or real-world performance optimization. Any physical aircraft work requires qualified adult supervision, legal compliance, and appropriate engineering review.

## Status
Concept and research portal. No flight-capable aircraft, validated flight model, or real-world performance is claimed.
